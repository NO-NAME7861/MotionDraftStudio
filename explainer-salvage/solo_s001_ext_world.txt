Ab ek baar phir rukkar us duniya ke baare mein thoda aur gehraai se samajh lete hain — kyunki is kahani ki asli taaqat sirf Jinwoo ke awaken hone mein nahi hai, balki us poori duniya ke tootne-banne mein hai, jo yeh sab dekhti hai.

Gates ka udai ek nayi economy ke saath hua tha. Pehle inhe "Dungeons" kaha gaya — lekin gates sirf gufaein nahi thi. Woh parallel worlds ke darwaaze the — har ek alag atmosphere, alag gravity, alag hazards ke saath. Ek gate mein volcanoes the, doosre mein frozen jheelein; ek mein hazaar saal purane ruins the, doosre mein modern factories jaisi abandoned labs — jaise kisi ne duniya ko game engine mein load kar diya ho. Aur in sab gates mein se sabse khatarnaak cheez thi — bina kisi warning ke achaanak khul jaana. Ek Monday ko aapke ghar ki deewar kagaz ki tarah phat jaati thi, aur andar se monsters bahar aa jaate the.

Hunters Association ka gathan inhi shuruati attacks ke baad hua. Government aur municipal council ne milkar ek legal framework banaya — kaun hunter hai, kaun nahi; kaun rank mein kahan baithta hai; kis gate ko clear karne ke liye kitna paisa milta hai; aur agar koi hunter civilian ko nuqsan pohanchaye toh kitni saza milegi. Dheere-dheere, bade guilds banne shuru hue — Korean Hunters Guild, Hunter's Guild, Scavenger Guild — har ek ke paas apne hunters, apne training centers, apne clinics, aur apni budgets the. Kuch guilds toh itne bade the ki unhe political parties se bhi compare kiya ja sakta tha.

Isi system mein rank E hunters ki halat — bilkul majdooron jaisi. Unhe dangerous raids par nahi bheja jaata tha, unhe "cleaning crew" ki tarah rakha jaata tha — monsters ke marne ke baad bodies hatane, basic scavenging karne, ya firefighting jaise chhote kaamon mein. Lekin agar kabhi koi bheed mein akela pad jaaye, ya kisi bade hunter ki release ho jaaye — toh E-rank bhi front line par bhej diye jaate the. Aur yahin par Jinwoo ki zindagi roz khatam hone ki kagar par thi.

Jinwoo ka ghar Seoul ke ek saste se ilaake mein tha — chhote-se apartment mein, jahan uska room uska sabse bada thikaana tha. Uski maa ko "Eternal Slumber" naam ki bimari thi — ek aisa disease jo monster attacks se juda hua tha, jisme mareez kabhi jaag hi nahi paate the — na uth sakte the, na hosh mein aa sakte the. Doctors ne saaf keh diya tha — ilaj ke liye magic ya high-end recovery treatment chahiye, jiski qeemat kisi E-rank salary ke bas mein nahi thi. Jinwoo ki source of income thi — baki hunters ki bodies saaf karna, medical-level jobs karna, ya kabhi-kabhi kisi khatre mein C-rank ki madad karke milne waali thodi si hazard pay.

Usi ghar mein usne gates par research papers bhi jama kar rakhi thi. Usko news clips yaad thi — kaise ek South Korean S-rank hunter "Hunters Guild" ke banner ke saath public parades mein khada hota tha; kaise "The Association" press conferences karti thi; kaise Japanese hunters ke baare mein rumors the — ki unke paas alag-alag aged gates ke liye alag-alag technology thi; aur kaise poori Asia ke major cities mein aaj bhi emergency sirens bajte the. Jinwoo ek scratched, purane-se notebook mein in sab mein se sabse zyada jis naam ko highlight karta tha, woh tha — "The Double Dungeon Incident". Lekin us waqt, us naam ka matlab use samajh nahi aaya tha ki yeh uski apni kahani banne wali hai.

Qismat uska saath tabhi deti, jab woh kisi C-rank party ke saath D-rank gate ki clearing ke offer par aakhri second tak haan nahi bharta. Lekin us din, woh zaroori tha — maa ke hospital bill ke liye, aur us akele lamhe ke liye, jab usne pehli baar mehsoos kiya ki koi aur aise weakness ko hathiyaar ki tarah use kar sakta hai.

Party meet point tha Gangnam ke paas ek purane se restaurant ke basement mein — jahan hunters aam taur par raid se pehle baithkar strategy banate the. Party leader ne sabko map dikhaya — gate number, D-rank, abandoned temple type; estimated time — 3 ghante; reward split — equal shares after Association deductions. Joohee ne kaha — "Meri fees toh ho jaayengi!" Party ka sabse josheela member hansa — "Bhai, paise se zyada zaroori hai — zinda lautna!" Jinwoo ne sirf muskurakar sar hila diya. Uske mann mein ek hi sawaal tha — "Kya aaj sab sahi jayega?"

Woh sawaal, jo hawa mein tairta hua apne jawab ka intezaar kar raha tha — uska jawab agle kuch ghanton mein milne wala tha, aur woh jawab tha — nahi, bilkul nahi.
