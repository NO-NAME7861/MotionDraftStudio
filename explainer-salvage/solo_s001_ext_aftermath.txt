Ab jab Jinwoo bach nikla aur Joohee ko hospital pahucha diya gaya, uske baad ki kahani ek quiet explosion ki tarah thi — bilkul chupke se shuru hoti hai, lekin poori duniya ko hila kar rakh deti hai.

Hospital mein Joohee ko dekha gaya — uske body mein kai non-responsive injuries thi, jinki koi explanation medical science se nahi mil rahi thi. The Association ka medical team turant is case mein involve hua — kyunki ordinary hunters ke paas toh iska jawab hi nahi tha. Lekin Joohee ne apni report mein bas ek hi baat likhi — "Sung Jinwoo ne mujhe bachaya."

Jinwoo ko hospital se ghar bhej diya gaya. Uske body par nishan the, jo kisi battle-torn survivor se zyada ek predator ki war scar ki tarah dikh rahe the. Uski maa — abhi bhi "Eternal Slumber" mein — use dekhkar bhi nahi jaag saki — lekin unke haath usi tarah pakde hue the, jaise woh hamesha use sambhalti thi. Jinwoo ne uske kamre mein mitti bhara ek naya phuldani rakh diya — local market se khareeda hua, saste se, par uska bhaav wahi tha jo kisi treasure se upar hota hai.

Agli subah, Hunters Association ke ek liaison officer uske ghar aaya — saade bag ke saath, sleeve par "A-RANK INVESTIGATION" ka badge — aur ek discreet voice mein pucha, "Sung Jinwoo ji, kal ke double dungeon mission ke baare mein aap se kuch sawaal hain."

Jinwoo ne sab kuch nahi bataya. Usne ordinary version diya — "Raaste mein patthar girne lag gaye, main peeche hat gaya, baki sab... kho gaye." Lekin Association ke officer uski aankhon mein woh chamak dekh rahe the jo ordinary E-rank hunters mein nahi hoti.

Liaison ne kaha — "Mr. Jinwoo, aap jaante hain ki aapke body scans mein jo mana signature mila hai, woh kabhi kisi E-rank mein nahi dekha gaya, hai na? Aap aaj tak jaante hain ki aapke andar kuch extraordinary hone wala hai." Jinwoo ne sar jhukaya — aur uski aankhon ke saamne System ne notification di — "HIDDEN QUEST AVAILABLE: Unfold your potential. Reward will be beyond your dreams."

Association ne Jinwoo ko consult ke liye Seoul ke ek specialized clinic bheja. Wahan ke senior doctors ne paya ki Jinwoo ka mana reservoir itna ghuna hai ki kuch ghanton mein hi measurable growth ho rahi hai — poori tarah se undetected, poori tarah se unnatural. "Yeh E-rank kaise ho sakta hai?" ek doctor ne pucha. Doosre ne jawab diya — "Shayad isi liye, kyunki yeh E-rank nahi hai."

Usi dauran, us poori duniya mein rumors failne lage ki double dungeon incident ke baad kuch special leaked intel saamne aaya tha — ek archaic sealing artifact — jiske baare mein governments bhi nahi jaanti thi. Hunters Association chupke se apni research labs mein is baare mein study karne lagi.

Joohee ko hospital se chutti mil gayi. Woh Jinwoo ke ghar aayi. Usne apne mailbox mein ek note payi thi — "Joohee — tum strong ho. Agli baar gate par jaogi, toh darr ki jagah determination lao. — Jinwoo" Joohee ko uski maa ke letters jaisi secure feeling hui.

Iske baad, Jinwoo ne apni life ke us phase mein kadam rakha, jahan ab uski days ko System decide kar raha tha. Morning exercise, solo training, gate research — sab kuch ab ek targeted mission ki tarah usne shuru kiya. Usne purane notes mein apni shuruati quests likh rakhi thi — level 1: survive; level 2: strengthen; level 3: uncover the truth about "The God" and his army.

Usi phase mein use pata chalta hai ki double dungeon ki ghaatna koi isolated nahi hai — yeh poori duniya ke gates ke peeche chupe ek ancient war ka sirf ek opening move thi. Jo shakti Jinwoo ne andar jagrit ki, woh ab use se poori tarah alag nahi ho sakti. Uske har saans, har ek decision — ab is System se bandha hua hai.

Hunters Association ne official statement jaari kiya — double dungeon mission mein hui casualties ke liye systematic investigation shuru ki gayi hai. Lekin jaise-jaise news leaks ho rahi thi, common log bhi Facebook groups aur news blogs mein is incident ke baare mein theories banane lage. Kuch logon ka manna tha ki yeh ek government experiment tha, kuch log maante the ki yeh kisi alien civilization ki tarah tha — lekin Jinwoo chupke se har hypothesis ko sunta raha aur apne andar muskurata raha, kyunki uske paas iska asli jawab tha.

Aakhir mein, us raat jab woh apne room mein baitha, System ki sabse badi notification aayi — "NEW HIDDEN DUNGEON DETECTED: A ranker's fate awaits. You are ready, Player? Let's see."

Jinwoo ne apni old dagger uthayi, uska naya shadow Igris uske saamne sway kar raha tha, aur usne System ke saamne apna official response diya — "Haan. Main ready hoon. Ab khel asli shuru hota hai."

Aur is tarah, us kamzor dikhne waale aadmi ne apni duniya mein sabse bada kadam rakha — jahan gates ka opening move ab poori duniya dekhne wali thi, aur jahan uska naam lene mein log ek baar rukkar sochenge.

Doston, Arc 1 ki yeh kahani yahin khatam nahi hoti — yahi sirf shuruat hai. Asli duniya toh abhi baaki hai. Yeh kahani humein sikhati hai ki sabse kamzor dikhna hi hamesha sabse badi taaqat nahi ho sakta — kyunki asli shakti aksar dikhne se bahar hoti hai.

Agle episode mein — "Hunters Association Arc" — Jinwoo apni pehchaan Jin-Ho aur baaki sabke saath saamne laayega; hum dekhenge ki kaise ek sabse weak player, dheere-dheere duniya ka sabse khatarnaak hunter banta hai — aur kaise baki S-rank hunters bhi usse agree hone lagte hain.
