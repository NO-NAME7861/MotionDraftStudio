Sannate mein khadi yeh temple ki sabse pehli cheez jo saamne aayi, woh thi murtiyon ki seedhi. Chhe vishal, kaali patthar ki murtiyaan — har ek poori building jitni badi, apne haathon mein kisi na kisi ajeeb cheez ko pakde hue. Kisi ki ungliyan lohaa ki zanjir mein phansi thi. Kisi ki ungliyan tooti hue patthar ke tukde par tikeyi thi. Aur bilkul beech mein, sabse unche sinhasan par — ek aisi murti thi, jiske baare mein sunne ke baad bhi bharosa nahi hota. Aankhein — do vast, laal-si chamakti hui aankhein — bina palak jhapkaye party ko ghoor rahi thi.

Party ka leader aage badha — shastra ki kitabon se parhi hui nahi thi, lekin darr samajh sakta tha. Usne sabko awaaz lagayi — "Sab shant raho, jaldi se peeche hatkar gate ki taraf chalo." Lekin jab tak sabne ghumke dekha, gate ki jagah ab ek solid, kaale patthar ki deewar khadi ho chuki thi. Woh jagah jahan se woh aaye the, woh ab mojood hi nahi thi.

Bahar ka raasta band tha. Party leader ne turant pehla order diya — hathiyaar tayyar, aur temple ki gehraai ki taraf daudo, shayad andar koi doosra raasta mile. Jo aaj yahan dikhaya, usse bachae bina bahar nikalne ka koi sawaal nahi tha. Joohee dar gayi thi, lekin medical student hone ke naate woh jaanti thi ki darna band karna zaroori hai — ghaav ki patti baandhne ke liye shant haath chahiye. Jinwoo — sabse kamzor — us ladki ki taraf dekha jo pehli baar gate raid par ja rahi thi, aur mann mein ek hi sawaal tha — "Kya main sachmuch kisi ko bacha sakta hoon, ya sirf kisi ke saath marne ke liye yahan hoon?"

Andar badhte hue hi sabko khayaal hota hai — zameen par tooti haddiyaan. Vishal haddiyaan. Aadam ke unche patthar ke gore se bani hui. Aur in haddiyon ki age — sadiyon purani. Iska matlab saaf tha — yahan pehle bhi insaan aaye the, aur yahan se koi wapas nahi gaya. Party ko ehsaas hone lagne laga ki yeh ek normal dungeon nahi, yeh kisi ki pariksha ki jagah hai — aur pariksha ki list unki names se bhari hui hai.

Pehle hamle ki jagah — ek vast, kaala-bhura Golem. Uski aankhein koi nahi thi, lekin uska poora body band mutthi ki tarah tana hua tha. Party ke sabse aage chal rahe ek B-rank hunter ne us par hammer chelaya — aur hammer toot gaya. Patthar ka body bina kisi nishan ke aage badha. Joohee ne peeche se healing magic ki koshish ki, lekin us kamre mein magic kamzor pad rahi thi — jaise kisi invisible shakti ne hawa se hi mana suck kar liya ho. Party ke baki members peeche hatate hue ek bade hall mein ghus gaye — aur woh hall tha is poori kahani ka "main hall of sacrifice".

Is hall mein chaar vishal altars the — aur har altar par ek shilalekh kuch is tarah ki warning ke saath khada tha — "Jo yahan aaya hai, use ek ki bali deni hogi. Warna sabki bali gayi." Shuruat mein sabne socha — bluff hai. Lekin kuch palon ke andar hi aage wale hall se khaufnaak gole gunjne shuru ho gaye, aur party ko samajh aa gaya ki yeh bluff nahi, yeh qanoon hai.

Party leader ne situation shaant rakhte hue sabko samjhaya — "Suno dhyaan se. Agar yahan sacrifice karna zaroori hai, toh sabse pehle strongest member... nahi..." usne rukkar kaha — "Jo bhi bali dega, woh is dungeon se... shayad bahar nikal jaaye."

Ab jo hua, woh mod is poori story ka sabse dark part hai. Party ke andar ke do members — jo pehle se hi scared aur self-preserving mindset wale the — unhone khud sacrifice hone ki bajaye apne kamzor saathiyon ko aage karne ki koshish ki. Ek member ne Joohee ko pakadkar react karne ka mauka hi nahi diya. Jinwoo — E-rank — jo hamesha chup aur background mein rehne waala tha — woh is baar aage badha, bina soche. Nahi, is baar woh us bhuke darr se aage nahi badh raha tha — is baar woh apni life nahi, balki ek beguunah ladki ki life bacha raha tha.

Aakhri dhakke mein, jab dono members Jinwoo ko altar ki taraf ghaseet rahe the, kamre ki roshni achaanak badal gayi. Main murti ne apni aankhein poori khol di — aur us pal mein sabko saaf dikh gaya ki yeh aam devtaa-bhagwaan ki kahani nahi hai. Yeh ek purane shakti hai, jo sadiyon se yahan bandi hai — aur usse taaze human suffering ka nasha hi jagrit rakhta hai.

Aur tab ek aawaaz — aisi aawaaz, jo hall ke har kone se ek saath gunji — "YOU ARE A PLAYER."

Jinwoo ki aankhon ke saamne achaanak koi cheez palat gayi. Jaise kisi ne uske andar ke darwaaze ko khol diya ho. Uski dhadkan ruki hui thi, aur usi pal, uski saari kamzori — sara mana-heen body — ek pal ke liye ek anjan lekin shaktishaali jhatke ke saath jaag gaya. E-rank Jinwoo — jise log kamzor samajhte the — ab ek aise Player ke roop mein jagrit hua, jiske andar ek System chal raha tha — ek aisa System, jo sirf use nazar aa raha tha.

Lekin shakti aane ka matlab yeh nahi tha ki woh jeet gaya. Us pal mein, saare patthar ke golems ek saath harakat mein aa gaye, surviving members ek ke baad ek jaagkar apne hi khoon mein tadapte gaye, aur hall ki deewarein gunjne lagi. Joohee behosh padi thi. Jinwoo — sabse kamzor aadmi — ab akela khada tha — theek us halat mein, jab baakiyon ne use chhod diya tha.

Agla part mein hum dekhenge ki kaise Jinwoo is purane temple ke saare monsters ka akele samna karta hai, ek nishchit chamatkaar ke saath uski life wapas li jaati hai, woh apni pehli shadow — Igris — ko jagrit karta hai, aur is dungeon se bahar nikalke duniya ke saamne ek nayi sachayi lekar khada hota hai. Bane rahiye.
