Party ne jab temple ke andar apne pehle kadam rakhe, toh kisi ko ehsaas tak nahi hua ki yeh sirf gehraai nahi, ek poori nayi duniya hai — ek ulta hua inverted world, jahan gravity ki direction koi aur hai, aur jahan hone waala har footstep ek gunj ke saath lautta hai — jaise koi invisible listener har word ko record kar raha ho.

Pehle hall mein deewaron par carvings thi — chehrein, haath, panje, aur aisi shapes jo kisi historical civilization se nahi, kisi nightmares ki language se aati thi. Joohee ne us language ko medical textbook ki kisi dead language jaisi samjhi — yeh kisi cult ki language thi, jo insaan ko "offerings" ki tarah use karta tha. Uske haath mein torch kanp gayi — lekin uski training ne usse wapas shaant kar diya.

Jaise-jaise party aage badhi, baki members ko mehsoos hone laga ki yeh jagah sirf purani nahi, active hai — jaise abhi bhi kuch zinda hai in deewaron ke andar. Party leader ne ek cautious call diya — "Sibbiro…" — woh shabd poora nahi nikla, kyunki tabhi aage se ek gunj aayi — jaise zameen ke andar se crore paga hihil rahe hon.

Party ke ek member — sabse tej, sabse confident — aage badh gaya. Kuch hi seconds baad uski awaaz aayi — "…andar kuch hai, chalo." Phir achaanak, ek bhaari dhhadakti hui aawaaz — aur woh member khoon se lathpath, par zinda wapas aaya. Uski chaati par gehri claw mark thi — jaise kisi patthar ki talwar ne uski ribs ko andar tak faad diya ho.

Ab party understand kar gayi — yeh temple unmanned nahi hai. Isme rakshak hain — aur woh bahut purane, bahut shaktishaali hain.

Joohee turant us member ke paas gayi aur use walls se support dekar letne ko kaha. Usne apna first-aid pouch khola — bandages, antiseptic, painkillers — lekin ghayal member ki aankhon mein kuch aur tha — darr, lekin usse bada ehsaas — koi cheez dekhne ka darr, jise aap shabdon mein nahi baandh sakte.

Party leader ne poori party ko ek line mein khada kiya aur kaha — "Hum yahin rukenge. Gate tak wapas chalne ki koshish karenge." Lekin jab tak dhamaaka hua, wapas jaane ka raasta bhi band ho chuka tha — Temple ki gehraai mein kuch aisa juda hua tha ki bahar nikalna ab ek ordinary raasta nahi tha — yeh ek trial ban chuka tha.

Ab jo agla hissa aata hai, woh mythology ka sabse kaala chapter hai. Party ko ek vast ceremonial chamber milta hai — jahan center mein ek vishal kaale patthar ka shilalekh hai, aur uske aas-paas chhote-chhote altar pieces — har ek par ek bloodstain ka nishan — jaise sadiyon se yahi kaam yahan hota aaya ho.

Joohee ne whisper kiya — "Yeh… manav bali ka sthaan hai." Uski voice kanp gayi. Party ka leader ab bhi situation ko control karne ki koshish kar raha tha, lekin andar se uska apna chehara darr dikha raha tha.

"Guests — purane samay mein — ek god ko khush karne ke liye, is jagah par fresh khoon se offerings di jaati thi — warna poori temple jagrit ho jaati thi. Yahan tak ki yahan ke stone statues — jinki aankhein abhi bhi khuli nahi thi — woh sirf ordinary kalaakritiyaan nahi, balki us god ki pehle faaisale ke gawah aur rakshak the."

Party ke do members — jo pehle se hi advance mein nervous aur self-preserving approach ke saath aaye the — unhone leadership ki baatein suni aur turant plan banane shuru ho gaye — "Agar offerings deni hi hain, toh kamzor ko kyun na dein? Kamzor aadmi ka koi contribution toh hai nahi." Unki aankhon mein us waqt ka ghrina-bhaav yaad rehne waala tha — ghrina, apne hi darr se jami hui.

Jinwoo sun raha tha — woh hamesha chupke se sab sunta tha, lekin is baar uski dhadkanen tez ho rahi thi. Joohee uski taraf dekh rahi thi — jaise woh us se protect karne ki umeed karti ho. Party leader ab tak faisla nahi le paaya tha — lekin tab tak faisla lene ka time bhi khatam ho chuka tha.

Tabhi, sabse center mein jo sabse badi murti thi — uski aankhein — woh laal, vishal, gussay se bhari — achaanak poori tarah khul gayi.

Chaaron taraf ki deewarein gunjne lagiin. Patthar ke golems — jo pehle distant halls mein so rahe the — ab ek-ek karke jaagne shuru ho gaye. Party ke peeche ka raasta — ek-ek karke — tootne laga. Woh chamber sirf ek kamra nahi tha — yeh ek trap tha, jise sadiyon se har us party ke liye tayyar rakha gaya tha jo is jagah par kadam rakhti. Aur aaj, us trap ne ek aur baar apna munh khol diya tha.

Party ke do members ne Joohee ko pakda — ki agar kisi ko bali dena hai, toh use hi dein. Jinwoo bharosa nahi kar pa raha tha — yeh apne hi saathiyon ki baghavat thi. Woh jaanta tha ki woh akele jo kar sakta hai, woh party mein sabse kam hai — lekin uske paas ek cheez thi, jo kisi ke paas nahi thi — darr ki kami. Woh Joohee ko chhodne ki bajaye aage badha — aur us ek kadam ne poori kahani ki direction badal di.

Aur yahin se kahani ka sabse tez, sabse khoaufnaak hissa shuru hota hai — jahan Jinwoo ki niyati badal jaati hai, jahan System jaagta hai, aur jahan ek kamzor aadmi apni poori body banakar bhi wapas lautta hai — ek naya janam lekar.
