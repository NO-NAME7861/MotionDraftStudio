Yahin par story apne sabse gehre andhere mein utarti hai — jahan koi helpline nahi, koi backup nahi, koi qaanoon nahi — sirf ek prachin qaanoon hai, jise mitane ki qeemat jaan se badi hai.

Jinwoo ne aage badhkar un do members ko jo Joohee ko pakde hue the, ek haathapaai mein dhakka diya — aur itne mein hi, hall ki chat se ek vishal chhaya utri. Woh ek Golem tha — nahi, woh murti se alag, ek zinda, rengta hua purana guard — jiske chehare par hazaar saal pehle kisi ki aakhri khushi jam gayi thi. Usne Jinwoo ko pakadke hawa mein uthaya — aur phir use khileyi haath jaisa sahlaaya — dheere-dheere, lekin nirdayata se, main altar ki taraf ghaseetne laga.

Joohee cheekh rahi thi — "Jinwoo! Chhodo use!" Uski aawaaz poori chamber mein gunj rahi thi — kisi pahaar ke beech jungle ki chillahat ki tarah.

Party ka leader ab tak kamre ke us kone mein khada tha — jaise kisi ne uske pair keel laga kar baandh diye ho. Woh jaanta tha ki ab koi heroic action kaam nahi aayega. Woh jaanta tha ki ab Jinwoo bali jayega. Aur woh ek imaandaar, lekin apni kamzori ke aage majboor insaan tha — jo poori ghaatna ko door se dekhta raha.

Main altar par Jinwoo ko rakha gaya. Murti ki aankhein ab poori tarah jal rahi thi — laal, ublta hua magma — aur uska chehara ab bilkul insaanon ke jaisa dekhne laga tha, magar ek corpse ki tarah, mrit atma ki tarah.

Kuch der tak Jinwoo chup raha. Phir usne aakhri baar apni aankhein uthaai — aur Joohee ki aankhon ko dekha. Woh aankhein jo use hamesha "kamzor, lekin bharosemand" maanti thi. Jinwoo ne dheere se sar hilaaya — jaise keh raha ho — "Pareshan mat ho. Main yahin rukta hoon."

Aur phir, main altar ke upar — murti ne apna haath Jinwoo ke seene par rakha — aur Joohee ne apni aankhein band kar liin, kyunki ab jo hone waala tha, woh dekhna kisi ke liye easy nahi tha.

Ek gunj — ek vibration — aisi ki poori chamber saath mein kanp gayi. Jinwoo ka body seedha pad gaya. Uski saansen — ek ek karke — thamne lagi — ab ruk chuki thi, ya ehsaas hi nahi hota. Joohee ki chekhaatein hawa mein gunjti rahi — lekin tab tak bahut der ho chuki thi.

System usi pal utha. Khaali chhaya us chamber mein uthti hai, aur usi ke beech — ek alokick, thandi aawaaz — "Player — your trial is over. Arise." Jinwoo ki aankhein — pehli baar — maut ke baad dobara khulte hain. Joohee jaagti nahi hai — lekin uski dhadkan abhi bhi chal rahi hai. Jinwoo dheere se apni aankhon ko hall ki chaaron murtiyon par daalte hai — aur ab, pehli baar, unhe ekdum jagrit mehsoos karta hai.

Aur yahin se — kahani ek aise pal mein kadam rakhti hai, jise kisi kitab, kisi anime, kisi paheli ne kabhi describe karne ki koshish nahi ki — jahan "mar kar wapas lautna" sirf ek metaphor nahi, ek haqeeqat hai.
