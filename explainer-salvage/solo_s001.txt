Aaj ki kahani hai us duniya ki jahan das saal pehle achaanak aasman se faatak khul gaye the, aur un faatakon ke peeche se monsters aane shuru ho gaye the — aur aaj ki asli kahani hai us aadmi ki, jise poori life sabne sabse kamzor kaha, aur jisne ek hi raat mein sabit kar diya ki asli taaqat sabse chupke aadmi ke andar dabi hoti hai. Chaliye shuru karte hain duniya se. Saal 2020 ke aas-paas ki baat hai — koi normal subah mein, duniya bhar mein achaanak portals nazar aane lage, inko "gates" kaha jaata hai. In gates ke andar kya tha? Vishal andhere bhulbhulaiyaan, purane khandar, zehrili jheelein, jhalti hui gufaein, aur in sabke beech khatarnaak monsters. Aur in gates se sabse pehle jo log nikle, woh kaun the? Hunters. Lekin yeh hunters koi film ke superheroes nahi the — inka majority aam logon ka tha, jinhe ek raat mein duniya ne ek nayi pehchaan de di. Kisi din, koi office jaane waala clerk ko uske body mein achaanak ek jagrit shakti mehsoos hui — use "awakened" declare kar diya gaya. Kisi housewife ke andar soyi hui shakti bahar aa gayi. Kisi college student ne pehle attack karne waale monster ko talwar se kaatke dekha, aur wahi uski zindagi ka turning point ban gaya.

Lekin har awakened ki shakti ek jaisi nahi thi. Isi liye Hunters Association ne ek ranking system banaya — sabse kamzor rank E se lekar bhagwan-level ke rank S tak. Rank E wahi tha jahan baki log salary wali job ke alawa kuch nahi kar sakte the. Rank C aur B wale active raids mein ja sakte the. Rank A wale rare gates clear karte the. Aur rank S? Rank S walon ki ginti gine-chune haathon par hoti thi — woh insaan se zyada kisi puraanik praani ki tarah maane jaate the.

Ab aate hain hamare protagonist par — Sung Jinwoo. Jinwoo ki kahani shuru hoti hai kisi mehal se nahi, kisi mysterious legacy se nahi, balki ek bilkul normal, garib ghar se. Uske papa ki death ho chuki thi, uski maa unki tabiyat theek nahi thi, aur ghar ka kiraya, bijli ka bill, aur maa ki dawaaiyan — yeh sab ek saath sar par aa khada tha. Jinwoo rank E ka hunter tha — matlab association ki nazar mein, uske haath mein ek purane-type ka dagger jaisa kuch tha, aur uske paas ek hi bharosa tha — thoda aur bachkar chalne ka. Woh group raids mein jaata tha, ghar se chhe ghante pehle nikalta tha, taaki "party" use pick karne aa jaaye. Wapsi par chawal aur daal ka simple khana, aur phir laptop par gates ki purani news padhna — yahi uska roz ka schedule tha.

Ab aapko lag raha hoga, yeh nayak jaisa nayak kaise hua? Jab har koi rank S ya A banne ka sapna dekh raha hai, yeh aadmi rank E par atka hua hai — waise chahe bhi, woh apni condition nahi badal sakta tha. Kya uski halat mein kami thi? Nahi. Woh strong nikalna chahta tha, bas uska mana bahut kamzor tha — jitna bhi try karta, studies batati thi ki woh normal se neeche ki range mein tha. Doosre hunters use "the weakest hunter of all mankind" bolkar bulaate the — hasta hua, mazaak mein. Lekin hamein mazaak nahi, dard mehsoos hota hai jab poore group ke saamne apni weakness wali baat sunni padti hai.

Jinwoo ko rank uthaana zaroori tha — maa ki dawaai ke liye, ghar ke kharche ke liye, aur sabse zaroori — apne self-respect ke liye. Isi liye jab ek local gate ka offer aaya — jiski clearing bahut simple batayi ja rahi thi, D-rank jaisa easy kaam, aur jiske liye ek C-rank party ko team chahiye thi — Jinwoo ne bina soche haan bhara. Party mein aur kaun the? Ek B-rank healer Joohee — medical college ki student, jiska ilaj ka skill uski sabse badi pehchaan thi. Party ka leader — ek anubhavi, shant-nature waala hunter, jisne kai dungeons clear kiye the. Aur baki C aur B rank wale chaar aur members — jo is pure raid ko ek routine Tuesday ki tarah le rahe the.

Raid simple tha — gate number late-night magic portal jaisa, dungeon type: abandoned temple style, difficulty D, dangerous monsters expected but manageable. Plan tha: andar jao, monsters clear karo, bahar niklo, paise baanto. Joohee internally soch rahi thi medical internship ke fees bhi cover ho jayenge. Jinwoo soch raha tha — pehli baar apni maa ko itna accha khana khilaaunga ki woh hans parhein. Party leader soch raha tha — yeh ek aur uneventful Tuesday hai. Yeh ek chhota hissa tha — andar ki gehraai poori duniya ki tarah phaili hui thi. Aur phir sabko asli sachayi ka ehsaas hota hai — jo gate inka clear kiya hua, woh is duniya ka regular dungeon tha hi nahi. Yeh ek trap tha — ek purana, puraana, aur sakht khoaufnaak niyam ke saath bana hua trap, jisme insaan ko sentence sunaane ke liye tayyar khada tha — chhe patthar ke devtaa ki murtiyaan. Aur in murtiyaan ki aankhein, jo ab tak khuli nahi thi — ab dheere-dheere khulne lagi thi.

Yahin se story ka poora direction badalta hai. Sawaalon ki jagah khauf khada ho jaata hai, aur himmat ki jagah survival instinct. Agla part mein hum dekhenge ki is purane temple ke andar asli mein kya hua, kaise party members ek-ek karke gayab hone shuru ho jaate hain, kaun jhooth bolta hai, kaun darr ka samna karta hai, aur Jinwoo — sabse kamzor aadmi — woh ek aakhri faasla kaise cross karta hai, jo koi aur nahi kar paaya. Bane rahiye — kyunki asli toh abhi shuru hone wala hai.

Ab ek baar phir rukkar us duniya ke baare mein thoda aur gehraai se samajh lete hain — kyunki is kahani ki asli taaqat sirf Jinwoo ke awaken hone mein nahi hai, balki us poori duniya ke tootne-banne mein hai, jo yeh sab dekhti hai.

Gates ka udai ek nayi economy ke saath hua tha. Pehle inhe "Dungeons" kaha gaya — lekin gates sirf gufaein nahi thi. Woh parallel worlds ke darwaaze the — har ek alag atmosphere, alag gravity, alag hazards ke saath. Aur in sab gates mein se sabse khatarnaak cheez thi — bina kisi warning ke achaanak khul jaana. Ek Monday ko aapke ghar ki deewar kagaz ki tarah phat jaati thi, aur andar se monsters bahar aa jaate the.

Hunters Association ka gathan inhi shuruati attacks ke baad hua. Dheere-dheere, bade guilds banne shuru hue — Korean Hunters Guild, Hunter's Guild, Scavenger Guild — har ek ke paas apne hunters, apne training centers, apne clinics, aur apni budgets the. Kuch guilds toh itne bade the ki unhe political parties se bhi compare kiya ja sakta tha.

Isi system mein rank E hunters ki halat — bilkul majdooron jaisi. Unhe dangerous raids par nahi bheja jaata tha, unhe "cleaning crew" ki tarah rakha jaata tha — monsters ke marne ke baad bodies hatane, basic scavenging karne, ya firefighting jaise chhote kaamon mein. Lekin agar kabhi koi bheed mein akela pad jaaye, ya kisi bade hunter ki release ho jaaye — toh E-rank bhi front line par bhej diye jaate the. Jinwoo ka ghar Seoul ke ek saste se ilaake mein tha — chhote-se apartment mein, jahan uska room uska sabse bada thikaana tha.
